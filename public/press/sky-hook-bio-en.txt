SKY HOOK

Sky Hook is an alternative rock band from Niš, Serbia, formed in 2023. The band's sound brings together melodic guitar lines, post-punk tension, surf rhythms and heavier textures. Their debut album Gde ptice lete, released in April 2025 through Pop Depresija / Zeleni Kačket, features 13 songs. Live appearances include Nišville, Čupin Rock Memorijal and club shows in Niš, Belgrade and Skopje.
